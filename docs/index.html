THE HONEST BOUNDARY

This is not a money machine.

Snapshots are asynchronous and may already be obsolete. Age measures proxy fetch time, not a guarantee of venue freshness. Bitstamp's snapshot time is separately checked. Kraken REST depth has per-level timestamps, not a whole-book timestamp.

Paper fills walk public depth after the selected delay, with visible costs. Two legs settle atomically in the model. Real partial fills, queue priority, leg risk, outages, borrowing, transfers, taxes and rebalancing are not reproduced. Profit here is not evidence of executable profit.

Public prices are requested only after Connect, every 15 seconds while visible. Six fixed requests per cycle. Third-party providers and the hosting service see requests/IP metadata. No exchange account, keys, money, personal data or saved trading history. Session data disappears on reload. The last 1,000 tape entries and P&L samples are retained in memory.
